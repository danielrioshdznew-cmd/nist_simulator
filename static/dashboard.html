What is what

Causes

Controller phases (c053)

Terms

board / episode
One simulated assembly task. A board is fixed by (seed, episode): the official conditions and both random streams follow from it, so every case replays exactly.
k
Number of pegs on the board (3-6). The time limit (horizon) is 280 × k steps; the board score is the mean over its k pegs.
seated
In its own hole, upright within 10°, bottom on the hole floor, the jaws above its top + 2 mm and not touching it, for 5 steps. Only pegs still seated at the end score.
peg score
0.75 + 0.25 × (0.85 × time left at its first seat + 0.15 × effort term); unseated = 0.
part_lost
A peg left the table (|x| or |y| > 0.30 m). The episode ends at once and every unseated peg scores 0.
squatter
A peg that starts on top of another hole's ring collar. Most are wedged and cannot be moved; the peg whose hole it covers is "blocked".
lying
A peg lying on its side. The gripper only turns about the vertical, so it would need a push/tip-up against a wall; c053 skips them.
estimate
What the policy sees: noisy, biased peg and hole positions (spheres/rings in the lab). The hand position is exact.
tier
Difficulty band of the board conditions (easy / medium / hard: clearance, friction, noise, delays).
control boards
Boards where c053 seats every peg; a sample of them checks that a fix doesn't break what already works.

Working on a fix with your local Claude

See CLAUDE.md in the repo. The loop is:

# 1. study a cause: watch cases in the lab, or dump exact traces
python tools/reproduce.py --case 123 --csv trace_123.csv
# 2. add a DEFAULT-OFF switch to policy/c053/src/refport_fx.py, then build it
python tools/build_policy.py --out policy/my_fix --set RF_MYIDEA=1
# 3. measure it only on that cause's failing boards + a control sample
python tools/evaluate.py --policy policy/my_fix --cause tip_pick --control 8
